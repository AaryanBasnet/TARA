Flying crew members are not allowed to import any

3 Nepali passenger traveling to the  foreign 
country:  
(1)  Passenger traveling foreign country can carry 
with him /her any or all items mentioned in 
subsection (1) of section 1. Customs duty will 
be exempted on such items. 
(2)  Nepali citizen traveling to foreign country can 
carry souvenirs produced in Nepal (except 
the banned or controlled items) up to the 
aggregate value of Rs. 50000/. Customs duty 
shall be exempted on such goods.
