TOPIC: Non-Tourist Visa - Working Visa
SOURCE: Visa Fee Documents

DESCRIPTION:
- Issued to foreign nationals only upon the recommendation from line agency (concerned Ministry of GoN), Labour Permit from the Department of Labour and Work Agreement from the Ministry of Home Affairs.

ISSUED BY: Department of Immigration

FEE (USD):
- 30 USD/Month: Foreigners working in offices or project directly affiliated with GoN and their dependents.
- 50 USD/Month: Foreigners and their dependants working in organizations having contract between donor country and the Nepal Government and funded by the donor country.
- 75 USD/Month: Foreigners and their dependents working in any organizations.
- Additional 20 USD for single re-entry facility.
- Additional 60 USD for multiple re-entry facility.

REQUIRED DOCUMENT:
- Recommendation letter
- Work / Labor Permit
- Work Agreement
- Appointment/ Contract letter
- Tax Clearance Certificate of the hiring organization
- Tax Clearance certificate of an applicant (for renewal)
- General Agreement with Social Welfare Council (NGOs/INGOs)
- Valid Passport
- Other supporting documents, if required

WHO CAN APPLY & HOW:
- Foreigners can apply from the online portal: https://nepaliport.immigration.gov.np/
