TOPIC: Customs Mobile Phones

RELATED QUESTIONS: How many mobile phones can I carry to Nepal?; Customs limit on the number of mobile phones; Can Nepali foreign employees bring an extra mobile phone duty-free?

ANSWER: Normally, you are allowed to carry only one mobile phone for your personal use. Nepali citizens in foreign employment and residing abroad for at least 6 months with prior approval from the labor ministry can bring one extra mobile phone without paying customs duty. You may be asked to complete customs process for the extra phone. For more information, visit https://customs.gov.np/
