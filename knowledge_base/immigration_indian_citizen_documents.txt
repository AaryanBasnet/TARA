TOPIC: Immigration Indian Citizen Documents

RELATED QUESTIONS: I am an Indian citizen? I am travelling to Nepal. What documents do I need to show at the airport?; Travel documents required for Indian citizens entering Nepal; Is Aadhar card or Driving license accepted for Indian nationals in Nepal?

ANSWER: You need to show a document that exclusively proves your nationality. Hence, you need to produce either a valid Indian Passport or Election Card (issued by the Election Commission) to enter into Nepal. Aadhar card or Driving license will not be accepted as a travel document.
