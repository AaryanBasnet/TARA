Chapter 11 
Arrangement for Demurrage 
50.  Arrangement for demurrage:  
(1)  No demurrage shall be charged for seven 
days from the date on which goods are 
stored in the customs office operated 
warehouse.  
(2)  In case goods are not cleared within the time 
limit prescribed in sub -rule (1) the demurrage 
shall be charged from the eighth day at a rate 
mentioned in Schedule 9. The demurrage 
shall not be more than the customs value of 
goods.  
51.  Remission for the demurrage  
(1) If the owner of the goods has reasonable 
ground for the remission of the demurrage, 
the owner may apply for th e remission with 
the evidence and documents to proof the 
claim to the chief of the customs office.
