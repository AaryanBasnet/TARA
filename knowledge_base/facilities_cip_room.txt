TOPIC: Facilities Cip Room

RELATED QUESTIONS: Does the airport have CIP Room facility?; Can Commercially Important Persons use a special room?; What is the CIP Room for?

ANSWER: Yes. This is available for Commercially Important Person (CIP) who on arrival may wish to rest while someone takes care of their formalities (i.e., customs, immigration, and luggage collection). You can avail this room as per the guideline of the office.
