TOPIC: Transport Dropoff Pickup

RELATED QUESTIONS: Is it possible to drop off and pick up someone in front of a terminal?; Can vehicles stop in front of the international terminal for drop-off?; Where can the general public pick up passengers?

ANSWER: Vehicles are permitted only to drop departing passengers in front of the international terminal. The general public can pick up their friends and relatives from the new arrival area of the international terminal. However, both pick up and drop off facilities are available for VIPs and Diplomatic personnel in front of the international terminal. Vehicles left unattended at the international terminal will be in police custody and fined.
