60.  Procedure regarding the issue of certificate of 
import:  
(1)  The person importing goods attracting sales 
tax or other taxes in the foreign country, may 
apply for gett ing certificate of import in the 
concerned customs office with the documents 
proving the import of such goods.  
(2) In case of receiving application pursuant to 
sub rule (1), such application should be 
examined and if it is justified, certificate should 
be issued in the format prescribed in schedule 
11.
