TOPIC: Non-Tourist Visa - Press Visa
SOURCE: Visa Fee Documents

DESCRIPTION:
- Issued for maximum of 4 months at a time to Journalists and Press representatives and their dependents.

ISSUED BY: Department of Immigration

FEE (USD):
- 15 USD/Month
- Additional 20 USD for single re-entry facility
- Additional 60 USD for multiple re-entry facility

REQUIRED DOCUMENT:
- Recommendation letter from the Department of Information
- Identity card issued by Nepal Press Council
- Valid Passport
- Latest Nepalese Visa, if any
- Other supporting documents, if required

WHO CAN APPLY & HOW:
- Journalists and Press representatives and their dependents can apply from the online portal: https://nepaliport.immigration.gov.np/
