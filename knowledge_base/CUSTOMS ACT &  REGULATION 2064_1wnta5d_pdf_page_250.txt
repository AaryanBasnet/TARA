66.  Opening or closing of customs office:  
(1)  The chief of the customs Office shall 
prescribe the opening or closing hours for his 
office and affix a notice thereof in the notice 
board of the office for the information of the 
public.  
(2) In case the Chief of the Customs Office feels 
that there exist special circumstances for the 
customers‟ interest o r excessive work 
necessitating the release of goods during a 
public holiday, or when office is closed, the 
Chief may keep it open and release the 
goods.
