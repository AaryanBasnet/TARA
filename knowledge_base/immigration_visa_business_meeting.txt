TOPIC: Immigration Visa Business Meeting

RELATED QUESTIONS: I am travelling to Nepal for a business meeting. What kind of visa should I get to enter Nepal?; Visa required for a business trip to Nepal; Can I get a Tourist visa for a business meeting?

ANSWER: You have to get a Tourist visa as an entry visa. You can get it either from Nepalese diplomatic missions abroad or On Arrival (if eligible). If you are investing and running a business in Nepal for the long term, you might consider getting a Business Visa.
