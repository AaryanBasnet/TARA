Chapter-13  
Provisions Relating to Punishment to Employees 
63.  Petition to be made: 
(1)  If one has a reasonable cause to believe that 
the Customs Officer or any employee of the 
Customs Office has done any act contrary to 
this Act or the Rules framed under this Act or 
has caused revenue loss or done any act 
guided by the sole motive to harass any 
importer or exporter, one may file a petition to 
the Director General in the case  of the 
Customs Officer and to the Customs Officer 
in the case of the employee of the Customs 
Office. 
(2)  The Director General or the Customs Officer 
shall examine any petition made pursuant to 
sub-section (1) and give information thereof 
to the petitioner.
