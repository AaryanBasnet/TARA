TOPIC: Customs Red Green Channel

RELATED QUESTIONS: Who can pass through Green channel or Red channel at the customs?; Difference between Green and Red customs channels; Which customs channel should I use if I have dutiable goods?

ANSWER: Passengers who do not have dutiable goods to declare can pass through the Green channel. Passengers who have dutiable goods to declare can pass through the Red channel. If a passenger chooses the Green channel with dutiable goods or prohibited items, their goods may be seized.
