TOPIC: Security Metal Detectors Pacemaker

RELATED QUESTIONS: Do I have to go through a walk-through metal detectors (WTMD)?; Do all passengers need to pass through the metal detector?; Can passengers with a Pace Maker bypass the WTMD?

ANSWER: Everyone must go through a walk-through metal detector except a passenger with a Pace Maker in their body due to a health problem. They can request the security person of their problem and can be checked manually. It is also advised the passenger with a Pace Maker not to pass through WTMD.
