BHAKTAPUR
Adjoining Kathmandu are other two ancient
cities of Patan and Bhaktapur which are
full of temples and rich architecture which
are just amazing and seriously breathe
taking. These three historic cities make the
Kathmandu valley, home to ethnic Newars
community of businessmen, farmers and
artists. The UNESCO has enlisted the
Kathmandu valley it in its world cultural
heritage site.
 
NAGARKOT
Nagarkot is known as the top spot for enjoying
the Himalayan panorama from the comfort of
your hotel room. On some days all eight
ranges are visible (Annapurna, Manaslu,
Ganesh Himal, Langtang, Jugal, Rolwaling,
Everest and Numbur). There is no guarantee
that they are not disappearing behind clouds
but the best time for clear skies is October to
December and March to April.
