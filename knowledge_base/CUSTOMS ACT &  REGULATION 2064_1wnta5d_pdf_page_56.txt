Provisions Relating to Examination of Goods 
28.  Power to open and examine consignment or 
packet: 
(1)  The Customs Officer may open and examine, 
or give order to any of his or her sub -ordinate 
employees to open and examine, each and 
every consignment or packet of any goods 
whatsoever to be exported or imported or 
open and examine them randomly and 
casually or open and examine only a certain 
percentage of the same. 
(2)  In making examination pursuant to sub -
section (1), the examination of live animals, 
perishable goods and such other goods as 
the Customs Officer considers necessary 
shall be made first.
