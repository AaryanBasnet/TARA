56.  Application to be made: 
Where the Customs Officer makes decision to 
suspend or cancel the license of a customs agent or 
to impose a fine on him or her pursuant to Section 
55, the customs agent who is not satisfied with that 
decision may file an application to the Director 
General within thirty five days after the date of such 
decision. The Dire ctor General shall make decision 
on such application normally within thirty days and 
such decision shall be final.
