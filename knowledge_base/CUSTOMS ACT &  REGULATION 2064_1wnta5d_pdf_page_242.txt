58.  Refund of the  excess amount of the customs 
duty or the penalty:  
(1) If the exporter or importer paid more than the 
due amount of customs duty for the export or 
import of goods or the penalty, the exporter 
or importer should apply for the refund of the 
excess amount at the customs office.  
(2)  In case of receiving application pursuant to 
sub-rule (1), such application should be 
examined and if it is found that the excess 
amount needs to be refunded, written 
decision should be taken to refund the 
excess amount and refun d such amount 
within fifteen days from the date of the 
application received.  
(3)  In case of refund of the amount pursuant to 
sub-rule (1), information )should be given to 
Director General with reasons for refund.
