CHITWAN NATIONAL PARK
Being the first protected area of Nepal, it has
a long history of over three decades in park
management and rich experiences in nature
conservation. Chitwan was a big game area
for the royal families, Rana rulers and their
guests. The UNESCO World Heritage Site
(1984) is especially renowned for its protection
of One Horned Rhinoceros, Royal Bengal Tiger
and Gharial Crocodile.
