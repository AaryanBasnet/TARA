DESCRIPTION:
- Issued to US students and their dependents with 5 years visa. Stay Limit for Study Period.

ISSUED BY: Department of Immigration

FEE (USD): 160 USD

REQUIRED DOCUMENT:
- Recommendation letter from concerned Ministry (recommendation for 5 years)
- University/College Admission Receipt
- Bank Statement (latest one week)
- Progress Report Card
- Valid Passport
- Other supporting documents, if required

WHO CAN APPLY & HOW:
- Applicant can apply from the online portal: https://nepaliport.immigration.gov.np/
