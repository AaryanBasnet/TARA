TOPIC: Reciprocity Visa - Working
SOURCE: Visa Fee Documents

DESCRIPTION:
- Issued to US citizens who are working in Nepal and their dependents with 3 years visa. Stay Limit for Working Period.

ISSUED BY: Department of Immigration

FEE (USD): 190 USD

REQUIRED DOCUMENT:
- Recommendation letter from the concerned Department/Ministry of GoN (recommendation for 3 years)
- Work permit from the Department of Labor
- Clearance from the Ministry of Home
- Appointment/Contract letter
- Tax clearance of hiring organization/institution/agency
- Tax clearance of applicant for renewal
- General Agreement with Social Welfare Council
- Valid Passport
- Other supporting documents, if required

WHO CAN APPLY & HOW:
- Applicant can apply from the online portal: https://nepaliport.immigration.gov.np/
