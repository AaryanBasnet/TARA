TOPIC: Immigration Visa Ineligible Countries

RELATED QUESTIONS: I am an Afghan citizen. I learnt that I need Visa prior to my Arrival to Nepal. What should I do?; Afghan citizens visa requirement for Nepal; How to get a visa for Nepal if I am not eligible for Visa On Arrival?

ANSWER: Please contact your closest Nepalese Diplomatic missions to obtain a Visa. Make sure you have a valid visa and passport before you board the plane.
Nationals from the following countries are not eligible for a visa on arrival in Nepal: Nigeria, Ghana, Zimbabwe, Swaziland, Cameroon, Somalia, Liberia, Ethiopia, Syria, Iraq, Palestine, and Afghanistan.
