RELATED QUESTIONS: I am an NRN card holder. How many days Gratis visa do I get?; Gratis visa duration for Non-Resident Nepali (NRN) card holders; How long is the free visa for NRNs?

ANSWER: Gratis visa to an NRN (Non-Resident Nepali) is issued for 30 days at the moment. It is subject to change as per the directive issued by the Department of Immigration and the Ministry of Home Affairs.
