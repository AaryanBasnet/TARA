21.  Valuation to be made: 
 After the examination of goods or documents 
pursuant to Section 20, the Customs Officer shall 
determine the customs value of the goods to be 
imported and of the goods to be exported under the 
provisions of Sections 13 and 16, respectively.

Officer shall confirm by taking base the 
subject mentioned in Sub -section (3) of the 
Section 61a. that whether classification is 
right or not.
