After breakfast, get into the waiting vehicle and proceed to Cable
Car station to board the Cable Car ride to famed Manakamana
Temple “Goddess of wish-fulfilling”. 
After the excursion return back to the Cable car station and head to
Chitwan National Park. 
After lunch, your naturalist will brief you about the wildlife activities
that you will be participating. 
In the evening, the well-stocked bar caters to the needs of the thirsty
and a hearty meal of continental or Nepalese cuisine will be served
around the campfire.
