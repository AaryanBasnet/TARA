53.  Procedure to issue notice:  
In case of issuing notice to the person related to the 
customs work or the person concerned with the 
customs as per section 82 of the Act, should provide 
fifteen days notice in addition  to the travel time and 
the notice should be issued in accordance with the 
procedure as prescribed in sub rule (2), (3), (4), (5), 
(6) or (7) of rule 52. 
However, regarding the notice issued as per this 
rule, sub rule (8) of rule 52 is not applicable.   
53A. Notice for the rightful claim:  
(1)  If the goods deposited in the customs office 
as per section 46 of the Act has owner not 
identified, before the goods are confiscated, 
notice should be issued in the notice board of 
the customs office if anyone has rightful claim 
on the goods. The claimant should be 
present with the application attaching the 
evidence of claim within fifteen days from the 
date of notice in addition to the travel time.
