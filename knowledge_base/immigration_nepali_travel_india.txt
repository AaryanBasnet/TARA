TOPIC: Immigration Nepali Travel India

RELATED QUESTIONS: I am Nepali passport holder. I am travelling to India. What should I do?; Documents required for Nepali citizens traveling to India; Can a Nepali citizen use a Citizenship Certificate to enter India?

ANSWER: Nepali citizens must produce a valid Passport or Nepali citizenship certificate or Election Card (issued by the Election Commission) to enter India. If traveling with an infant (24 months), you may be asked for a birth certificate, and if traveling with a kid below 16 years, you may be asked for either a valid school ID with a birth date or a valid minor identity card from the Nepal Government.
