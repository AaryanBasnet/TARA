(9)  In case airline company registered to operate 
airlines have to export engines of airplane or 
helicopter for repair and during the period of 
repair if the airway company has to import 
engine on rent from the foreign country, the 
company may import engine on bank 
guarantee equivalent to Customs duty in the 
Customs office and such import should be for 
maximum period of six months. If the engine 
is ex ported within the period, the bank 
guarantee will be released. If the engine is 
not exported within the period, the Customs 
duty should be charged treating engine as 
fresh import.  
(10)  Notwithstanding anything contained in this 
rule, on the recommendatio n of the Ministry 
of Foreign Affairs, the diplomatic mission may 
export to repair goods and import after repair 
without deposit and on record.
