TOPIC: How to get SIM card
SOURCE: General Advice

you can buy a physical SIM card at Tribhuvan International Airport (TIA) from stalls for major providers like Ncell and Nepal Telecom (NTC), located just past the money exchange counter and before the exit. You will need to have your passport and passport-sized photos ready for registration, as the process is required for activation. For potentially faster and more flexible options, consider purchasing an eSIM before you arrive, ensuring your phone is compatible and unlocked.
