Visas are required for all foreigners wishing to enter Nepal, except for Indian nationalities, who need to show their identity at the counter to identify themselves.

There is a special counter for VIPs/Diplomats.

It is advisable that you fill up the disembarkation card in the aircraft itself before disembarking to avoid wasting time filling it at the arrival counter.

TIA Immigration can grant you a VISA for 90 days. For further details, you can contact the Immigration Office at Dillibazar, Kalikasthan in Kathmandu.

After visa formalities are completed, you have to go downstairs to collect your luggage at the custom area.

You can make a local call from the telephone booth behind the Immigration counter before descending the steps into the Custom area.

You need to get your hand baggage examined through the X-ray machine before your exit to the arrival lounge.

Free baggage trolleys are available.
