DESCRIPTION:
- Issued to US citizens and their dependents with 5 years visa duration with provision of 180 days stay per year.

ISSUED BY: Department of Immigration

FEE (USD): 160 USD

REQUIRED DOCUMENT:
- Current Bank statement having minimum balance of USD 10,000
- Health Insurance Certificate
- Police Report
- Other supporting documents, if required

WHO CAN APPLY & HOW:
- Applicant can apply from the online portal: https://nepaliport.immigration.gov.np/
