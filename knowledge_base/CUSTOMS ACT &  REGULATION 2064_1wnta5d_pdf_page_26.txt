10.  Power to accord bonded warehouse facility: 
The Government of Nepal may, if it considers 
appropriate to accord the bonded warehouse facility 
to any person desirous of availing of the bonded 
warehouse facility, so accord the facility, as 
prescribed, that the bonded warehouse is operated 
subject to the terms as prescribed.
