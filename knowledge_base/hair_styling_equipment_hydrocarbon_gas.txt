- **Description:** Hair styling equipment containing a hydrocarbon gas cartridge, up to one (1) per passenger or crew- member, provided that the safety cover is securely fitted over the heating element. This hair styling equipment must not be used on board the aircraft. Spare gas cartridges for such hair styling equipment are not permitted in checked or carry-on baggage.
