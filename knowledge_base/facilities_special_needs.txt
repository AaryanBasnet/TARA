TOPIC: Facilities Special Needs

RELATED QUESTIONS: What are the facilities available for passengers with special needs?; Is there priority queue for elderly or disabled passengers?; Barrier-free access for special needs passengers

ANSWER: Kathmandu Airport offers barrier-free access for passengers with special needs. There are arrangements for a separate priority queue for elderly people, parents with infants, pregnant women, and physically challenged persons. For assistance, you are requested to contact your respective airlines in advance. You may contact the help desk or information desk for further query.
