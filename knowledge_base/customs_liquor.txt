TOPIC: Customs Liquor

RELATED QUESTIONS: How much liquor is allowed as duty free while travelling to Nepal?; Duty-free allowance for alcohol in Nepal customs; Limit on liquor carried into Nepal without paying duty

ANSWER: You are allowed to carry one litre of any type of alcoholic liquor (whiskey, wine, vodka, etc.) OR 12 cans of beer without paying customs duty. Amounts exceeding this limit are subject to customs duty. For more information, visit https://customs.gov.np/
