(4)  The Government of Nepal may accord partial 
or full customs duty exemption to the goods

(5)  The Government of Nepal may accord partial 
or full customs duty exemption to the fuel to 
be consumed during international flight, 
engine of aircraft, spare parts, machine, 
equipment thereof, food, liquors, beer and 
light drinks consumed in flight by an 
international air service company. 
(6)  The provisions for according the duty facility 
to any goods to be brought again into Nepal 
from any part of Nepal via any abroad route 
shall be as prescribed. 
(7)  The provisions for according the duty facility 
to any goods to be sent again to a foreign 
country from the foreign country via Nepal 
shall be as prescribed.
