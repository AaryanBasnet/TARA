TOPIC: Immigration Visa Multiple Reentry

RELATED QUESTIONS: I need Tourist Visa with Multiple Re-entry facility. What can I do?; Does the Nepal Tourist Visa have a multiple re-entry facility?; Can I get a multiple re-entry visa on arrival?

ANSWER: All Tourists Visas issued by Nepalese diplomatic missions and 'On Arrival' at the Entry & Exit points (including the Immigration office, Tribhuvan International Airport) bear multiple Re-entry facility.
