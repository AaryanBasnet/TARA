TOPIC: Facilities Accommodation

RELATED QUESTIONS: Is there any accommodation facility in and around the airport?; Can I find a hotel near the airport?; Are there lodging options inside the airport?

ANSWER: Accommodation facility within the airport is not available. However, Hotel facilities are available at 4 minutes walking distance from the airport terminal. Other hotels are available in the downtown city located four kilometers away. Please check the website and book directly with the hotels.
