The Nepali Citizens traveling abroad for Institutional purposes (e.g., Seminars, Exhibitions, Knowledge and Skill exchange programs, etc.) shall produce the following documents at the departure desk of immigration:
* Passport valid at least for 6 months.
* VISA of the inviting country.
* Invitation letter and program details explaining the purpose of the visit.
* Two-way air ticket.
* A document clarifying the institutional association.
* Other documents as appropriate and requested.
