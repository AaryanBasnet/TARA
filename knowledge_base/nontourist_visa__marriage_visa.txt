DESCRIPTION:
- Issued to foreigners married to Nepali citizen.

ISSUED BY: Department of Immigration

FEE (USD):
- 15 USD per month
- No Visa fee till marriage relation for foreigners who took visa free of cost according to Foreign Relation Regulation, 2032.
- Additional 20 USD for single re-entry facility.
- Additional 60 USD for multiple re-entry facility.

REQUIRED DOCUMENT:
- Valid Passport
- Marriage Certificate issued by District Administration Office or District Court. If marriage registered abroad; Marriage Registration Certificate and verification letter from concerned embassy.
- Citizenship Certificate of Nepali spouse
- Physical presence of Nepali spouse
- Marriage registration certificate from concerned local level (in case of court marriage, marriage done from diplomatic missions from/in Nepal and marriage done in abroad between nepali and foreign citizen).
- Marriage certificate from local level

WHO CAN APPLY & HOW:
- Applicant can apply from the online portal: https://nepaliport.immigration.gov.np/
