RELATED QUESTIONS: What is the cash limits in Nepal customs?; Maximum cash amount a foreign citizen can take out of Nepal; Export limit for foreign currency from Nepal

ANSWER: Foreign citizens are not allowed to take with them more than US Dollar 5000 or equivalent in other foreign currency in cash when returning from Nepal, but there won’t be an obstacle to take the money within the limits of the self-declared amount while entering Nepal.
