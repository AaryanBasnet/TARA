87.  Duty to render assistance and help: 
If, in the course of exercising powers and performing 
duties under this Act or the Rules framed under t his 
Act, the Customs Officer or other employee asks the 
police and other governmental employee for any 
kind of assistance or help, it shall be the duty of 
such police or employee to render such assistance 
or help. 
88.  Other body not to intervene: 
No authority or body shall, in relation to any goods 
remaining within the Customs Office for export or 
import, make any kind of intervention until such 
goods are cleared by that Office and removed from 
the customs area.
