69.  Compensation for goods withheld in Customs 
Office or godown: 
(1)  The Customs Office  or the godown shall so 
safely keep the goods held in the Customs 
Office or the godown that no loss or damage 
is caused to such goods. 
(2)  If the goods referred to in sub -section (1) are 
stolen, lost or otherwise destroyed, damaged 
or get damaged, except for a wear and tear 
due to a natural calamity or accident or wear 
and tear likely to arise normally in the course 
of holding or lifting goods, the owner of such 
goods shall be entitled to recover, as 
prescribed, compensation for such goods
