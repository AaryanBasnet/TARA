5.  Formality to be fulfilled regarding duty privilege 
for sending Goods from one part of foreign 
country to another part of foreign country 
through Nepalese territory : 
(1)  In case of sending Goods from one part of 
foreign country to another part of foreign 
country through Nepalese territory, the owner 
of goods should submit an application in the 
Department of Customs specifying the 
reasons for using Nepalese territory, export 
and import Customs point and descript ion of 
particulars of goods.
