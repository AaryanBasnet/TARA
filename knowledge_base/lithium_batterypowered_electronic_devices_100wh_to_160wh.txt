TOPIC: Lithium battery-powered electronic devices (100Wh to 160Wh)
SOURCE: Forbidden Items

- **Description:** Lithium-ion batteries for portable (including medical) electronic devices, a Wh rating exceeding 100 Wh but not exceeding 160 Wh. For portable medical electronic devices only, lithium metal batteries with a lithium content exceeding 2 g but not exceeding 8 g.
- **Operator Approval Required:** YES
- **Permitted in Checked Baggage:** YES
- **Permitted in Carry-on Baggage:** YES
