91.  Power to take action: 
If any person arrests any other person while 
bringing goods through elsewhere than the customs 
area by evading the customs duty and produces 
such other person for action under this Act, the 
Director General or the official deputed by him or 
her, and if such goods produced to any customs 
office customs officer concerned, shall have the 
power to take action against such person. 
92.  Power to frame Rules: 
(1)  The Government of Nepal may frame Rules 
in order to implement the objectives of this 
Act. 
(2)  Without prejudice to the generality of sub -
section (1), the Government of Nepal may 
frame rules of origin in relation to goods to be 
exported or imported.
