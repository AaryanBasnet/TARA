TOPIC: Terminal Arrangements

RELATED QUESTIONS: What are the arrangements at the terminal?; How many gates are available for entry into the check-in area?; Number of boarding gates at the international terminal

ANSWER: For passenger convenience, the following arrangements have been made at the International Terminal:

For the entry into the check-in area:
* Four gates (A1, A2, B1, and B2) are available at the canopy area of the terminal to enter passengers into the check-in area. You need to show your ticket and passport to the security personnel.

At the Check-in area:
* Ticket check-in counters are available in two parts: A-side (A01 to A13) and B-side (B01 to B21). The counter allocation is dynamic (CUTE system), so check the airline counter signage. A fixed self-check-in kiosk machine is available for passenger ease in B-side (initially only for Malaysian airlines).

At the departure immigration:
* A total of 14 immigration desks, including one for VIP/Diplomatic, official, and needy passengers, are available. Queue managers ensure proper alignment and social distancing.

At the Boarding Gate:
* Seven Boarding gates are available. Passengers are recommended to sit according to their boarding gate number mentioned on the boarding pass and closely monitor public announcements.
