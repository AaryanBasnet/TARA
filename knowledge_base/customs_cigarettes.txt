TOPIC: Customs Cigarettes

RELATED QUESTIONS: How many cigarettes can I bring?; What is the cigarette limit?; Tobacco allowance Nepal

ANSWER: You are allowed to bring the following tobacco products duty-free: 200 cigarettes (one carton), OR 50 cigars, OR 250 grams of tobacco. This allowance is per person aged 18 years or above. Amounts exceeding these limits are subject to customs duty.
