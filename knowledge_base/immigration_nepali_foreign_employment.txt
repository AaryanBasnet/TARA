TOPIC: Immigration Nepali Foreign Employment

RELATED QUESTIONS: I am Nepalese. I am travelling for foreign employment. What should I do for immigration clearance?; Documents required for Nepali foreign employment travelers at immigration; Is a labor permit required for Nepalese foreign workers?

ANSWER: You must present the following documents to an immigration officer in the departure desks:
* Valid passport and Visa.
* Boarding pass to the final destination.
* Labor permit issued by the Government of Nepal.
* Other documents as appropriate and requested.
After immigration checks, proceed to security checks. Make sure your departure is certified in your boarding pass and travel document (Passport).
