DESCRIPTION:
- Issued to investors and businessmen/women investing and undertaking business in Nepal.

ISSUED BY: Department of Immigration

FEE (USD):
- 20 USD/Month, 200 USD/Year, 500 USD for 5 Years: Foreigner and their dependants having an investment more than NRs 100 million.
- 35 USD/Month, 400 USD/Year, 1000 USD for five years: Foreigner and their dependents having an investment equal to or less than NRs 100 million.
- Free of Cost for NRS 1000 Million Investment.

REQUIRED DOCUMENT:
- Recommendation letter from Department of Industry or concerned line agency of Government
- Bio Data (for first time applicants)
- Foreign Investment Acceptance letter from the Department of Industry
- Copy of Company Registration Certificate
- Copy of PAN/VAT certificate
- Copy of Share certificate
- Copy of Industry Registration Certificate
- Copy of Passport and latest Visa
- Latest tax clearance certificate
- Industry Monitoring and Supervision report
- Progress report of company of industry (for renewal)
- Valid Passport
- Other supporting documents, if required

WHO CAN APPLY & HOW:
- Applicant can apply from the online portal: https://nepaliport.immigration.gov.np/
