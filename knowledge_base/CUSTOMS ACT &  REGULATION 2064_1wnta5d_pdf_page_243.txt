59.  Procedure for providing copy of documents to 
the concerned person:  
(1)  The concerned person may apply to the 
customs officer for providing a copy of any 
documents,  
(2)  Pursuant to sub rule (1), the applicant should 
specify the description of the documents, the  
purpose for providing such document, and 
the evidence for being the concerned person.  
(3)  In case of receiving application pursuant to 
sub rule (2), such application should be 
examined and if it is justified, the customs 
officer should provide copies of  the 
documents to the applicant within three days 
of the date of application by charging fees as 
per sub rule (4).
