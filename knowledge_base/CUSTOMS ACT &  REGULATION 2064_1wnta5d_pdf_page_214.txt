38. Appointment of customs agent:  
(1)  Any exporter or importer, being present in the 
customs office, may clear the goods or other 
works relating to customs.  
(2) In case the exporter or importer is 
government institution, fully or partially owned 
public enterprises or institution , the Board of 
Director or the Chief Executive o fficer or 
administrative chief of such institution may 
decide to appoint a staff of the institution to 
be present at the customs office to clear the 
goods or do some other customs related 
function.
