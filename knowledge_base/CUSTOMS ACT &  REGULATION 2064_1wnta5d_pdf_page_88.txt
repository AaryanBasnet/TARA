53. Customs agent to be deemed owner of goods:  
If the owner of any goods appoint any person as his 
or her customs agent to get such goods cleared 
from the Cu stoms Office or to do any other act as 
referred to in this Act and the Rules framed under 
this Act, such customs agent shall, for that purpose, 
be deemed to be the owner of such goods. 
53A. Duties of Customs Agent:  
Duties of Customs Agent, appointed pursu ant to this 
Act for the purpose of releasing goods from the 
Customs Office or to do other works relating to 
customs office, shall be as follows: 
(a)  To fully comply with the laws related with 
customs, 
(b)  To provide necessary support to the customs 
office to simplify customs clearance process, 
(c)  To provide necessary support to the customs 
office in duty recovering work, 
(d)  Not to cause any kind of obstruction in work 
and activities of the customs office,
