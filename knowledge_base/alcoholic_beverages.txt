TOPIC: Alcoholic beverages
SOURCE: Forbidden Items

- **Description:** Alcoholic beverages, when in retail packagings, containing more than 24% but not more than 70% alcohol by volume, in receptacles not exceeding 5 L, with a total net quantity per person of 5 L.
- **Operator Approval Required:** NO
- **Permitted in Checked Baggage:** YES
- **Permitted in Carry-on Baggage:** YES
