TOPIC: Non-Residential Nepali Visa
SOURCE: Visa Fee Documents

DESCRIPTION:
- Issued to the NRN card holders.

ISSUED BY: Department of Immigration

FEE (USD): Free of cost

REQUIRED DOCUMENT:
- NRN Card(Original) issued by Ministry of Foreign affairs and its mission based abroad
- Valid Passport
- Photocopy of Passport and
- Latest Nepalese Visa
- Other supporting documents, if required

WHO CAN APPLY & HOW:
- Applicant can apply from the online portal: https://nepaliport.immigration.gov.np/
