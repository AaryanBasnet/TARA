DESCRIPTION:
- Issued to a person who is on an official visit to Nepal and the family members (spouse and children) of the person.

ISSUED BY:
- Department of Consular Services
- 30-Days entry visa is issued by Embassies of Nepal or by Immigration offices at entry points.

FEE (USD): Free of Cost

REQUIRED DOCUMENT:
- Valid Official Passport
- Diplomatic Mission Note and Recommendation
- Other supporting documents, if required

WHO CAN APPLY & HOW:
- Officials and their dependents can apply to the Department of Consular Services via: https://nepalconsular.gov.np/diplomatic/public/
- Officials and their dependents with Official passports can apply for 30 days entry visa from the online portal: https://nepaliport.immigration.gov.np/
