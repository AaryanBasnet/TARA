82.  Mode of issuance of notice: 
Except where separate provisions are made in this 
Act and the Rules framed under this Act, the 
Customs Office shall, while issuing a personal or 
public notice in the name of a person who has or 
may have concern with the activities of the Customs 
Office, issue a notice, as prescribed, by giving a 
period of fifteen days. 
83.  Delegation of powers: 
(1)  The Government of Nepal may, by 
notification in the Nepal Gazette, delegate 
any or all of the pow ers conferred to it by this 
Act or the Rules framed under this Act to the 
Director General or any other official 
prescribed in that notification. 
(2)  The Director General may, as required, 
delegate some of the powers conferred to 
him or her by this Act to  any subordinate 
officer employee.
