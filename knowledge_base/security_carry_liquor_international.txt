TOPIC: Security Carry Liquor International

RELATED QUESTIONS: Can a passenger travelling on International flight carry liquor?; Liquor purchased at the airport for international travel; Does duty-paid liquor need to be sealed in a STEB bag?

ANSWER: Passenger can purchase Liquor from duty paid shops at the airport. It should be sealed packed and should be STEB (Security Tamper Evident Bag) packaging as per ICAO regulations.
