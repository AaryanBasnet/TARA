TOPIC: Customs at Arrival
SOURCE: Arrival Info

There are two channels for customs clearance: the Green Channel and the Red Channel.

Green Channel:
Passengers not having any dutiable items can proceed to the Green Channel.
Passengers having the following items (within the specified limits) may use the Green Channel:
- Gold ornaments not more than 50 gms
- Silver ornaments not more than 500 gms
- Used linen
- One camera
- One pair of binoculars
- One movie/video camera
- One radio
- One tape recorder
- 15 tape cassettes
- 10 disc recorders
- 15 rolls of photo films
- 10 rolls of movie camera films
- One tricycle
- One perambulator
- One wristwatch
- Necessary medicines
- Ordinary packed food worth not more than NRs 10,000
- Fresh fruit worth not more than NRs 1,000

Red Channel:
If a passenger is in doubt about dutiable or non-dutiable items, he/she can proceed to the Red Channel.

Currency Declaration:
Passengers should declare foreign currency if they have more than US$ 5,000 or its equivalent in any convertible currency.

Rules for Electronic Equipment:
All-electric equipment, TV, Video Camera, Laptop, etc. should be either duty paid or endorsed on your passport so that it can be carried back to your country after completing your stay.
