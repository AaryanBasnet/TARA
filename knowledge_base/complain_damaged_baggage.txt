Lost luggage should be reported immediately at the airport upon arrival before leaving the baggage area. Concerned airline or Nepal Airlines staff will contact the passenger when the luggage has been located. Any damage should also be reported immediately. Requests for financial compensation can be submitted to concerned airlines or the NAC Customer Services Department Relations in the case of Nepal Airlines Corporation. If you have travel insurance, you can submit your claim to your insurance company along with the report form received from the baggage claim area.
