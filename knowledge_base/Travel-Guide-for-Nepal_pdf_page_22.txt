Upon arrival get into a waiting vehicle and drive to Suikhet Phedi
(Trekking starting point), after a short preparation start trekking to
Dhampus. 
There are magnificent views of the mountains from west to east of the
Annapurna South (7,219m), Hiunchuli (6,441m), Mardi Himal (5,587 m)
and Macchapucchare (6,993m) to the north. 
Day 6:  Fly to Pokhara
Wake up for a breathtaking sunrise view over the Annapurna
Himalayas & enjoy an outdoor breakfast. Shortly afterward start
trekking Maajgaun. 
After leaving the village, we continue on the trail going down a fairly
gentle slope cutting into steps in the middle of the forest and then a
short climb will bring us to the settlement of Pothana (2,000m) after
an hour with magnificent views of the western Himalayas. 
Upon arrival check-in at a Mountain Lodge for lunch.
