TOPIC: Terminal Entry Time

RELATED QUESTIONS: How soon can a passenger enter the International terminal?; When can I enter the international terminal before my flight?; Check-in commencement time before departure

ANSWER: On the date of travel, a passenger can enter the terminal anytime. But due to the congestion of the terminal, normally 3 hours before the flight. The check-in commencement time is usually 3 hours prior to departure and the ticket checking counters close at least one hour prior to departure.
