DESCRIPTION:
- Issued to a person holding a diplomatic passport issued from any country and recommended from the concerned diplomatic mission for the diplomatic visa.
- Issued to a person considered eligible to grant diplomatic privileges by Government of Nepal (GoN) and the family members of such person holding the diplomatic passport.

ISSUED BY:
- Department of Consular Services
- 30-Days entry visa is issued by Embassies of Nepal or by Immigration offices at entry points.

FEE (USD): Free of Cost

REQUIRED DOCUMENT:
- Valid Diplomatic Passport
- Diplomatic Mission Note and Recommendation
- Other supporting documents, if required

WHO CAN APPLY & HOW:
- Diplomats and their dependents with diplomatic passports can apply to the Department of Consular Services via: https://nepalconsular.gov.np/diplomatic/public/
- Diplomats and their dependents with diplomatic passports can apply for 30 days entry visa from the online portal: https://nepaliport.immigration.gov.np/
