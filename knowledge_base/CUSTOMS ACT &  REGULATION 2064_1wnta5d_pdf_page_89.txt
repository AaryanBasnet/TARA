54.  Customs agent to be responsible: 
If any customs agent appointed pursuant to Section 
52 does any act contrary to this Ac t or the Rules 
framed under this Act, thereby causing any loss and 
damage to the owner of goods, such agent shall pay 
an amount equal to that loss to the owner of such 
goods, as prescribed.
