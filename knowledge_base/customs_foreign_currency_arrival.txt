TOPIC: Customs Foreign Currency Arrival

RELATED QUESTIONS: What is the rule of carrying foreign currency?; Maximum foreign currency allowed to carry into Nepal without declaration; Declaration requirement for foreign currency above US Dollar 5000

ANSWER: Nepalese or foreign citizens can carry foreign currency worth US Dollar 5000 (Five Thousands) or equivalent other foreign currency when coming to Nepal. If carrying more than this amount, you must self-declare (as per the Anti-Money Laundering Act, 2064) at customs.
