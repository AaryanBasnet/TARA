TOPIC: Security Valuables In Baggage

RELATED QUESTIONS: Can a passenger travelling on International flight carry valuable goods in their check-in luggage?; Is it allowed to put valuables like gold in checked luggage?; Rules for carrying ornaments/money in checked baggage

ANSWER: No, passengers are not allowed to carry valuable goods (e.g., ornaments, gold, silver, money, etc.) in their checked-in baggage/luggage. However, they can carry it in hand baggage with the prior approval from customs and declaration from the passenger at the airline checking counter during ticket checking.
