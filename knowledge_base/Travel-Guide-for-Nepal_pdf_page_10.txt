Dal is a soup made of
lentils and spices, served
over boiled grain, bhat—
usually rice but sometimes
another grain - and a
vegetable curry, tarkari.
DAL-BHAT-TARKARI
Nepalese dumplings are
usually filled with steamed
vegetables or meat and
encased in flour-based
dough that is then steamed
or fried. Commonly eaten
as snacks, momo are served
with a delicious dip that
can be strong and spicy.
MOMO
The traditional homemade,
sweet, ring-shaped rice
bread is quite famous in the
festivities.
SEL ROTI
A Yomari is a flavorful
Nepalese rice flour
dumpling stuffed with
molasses. It is prepared in a
triangular (or teardrop)
shape which is then
steamed and served hot.
YOMARI
