TRIBHUVAN INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT

Brief Description
Tribhuvan International Airport is located at Kathmandu, the capital of Nepal, Bagmati Province and
serves as the only international airport. This airport is the busiest airport in terms of passengers'
movement. It is the only air-gate to Nepal from international destinations and links with all domestic
airports.
