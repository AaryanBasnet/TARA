TOPIC: Transport Between Terminals

RELATED QUESTIONS: How do I reach domestic terminal from international arrival?; Distance between international and domestic terminals; Is there a shuttle bus from international to domestic terminal?

ANSWER: The domestic terminal is approximately 500 meters north of the international terminal. On collecting the baggage, passengers can exit the arrival hall and wait for the shuttle bus which will connect the passenger with the domestic terminal free of cost. The shuttle bus will arrive in every 20 minutes. Kindly ask the security person in that area for help. For further assistance, you can visit https://www.tiairport.com.np/
Shuttle buses are provided which connect passengers between the Domestic and International Terminal and also connect to the city bus stop to go to the downtown city.
