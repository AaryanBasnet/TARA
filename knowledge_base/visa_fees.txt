TOPIC: Visa Fees

RELATED QUESTIONS: How much does a tourist visa cost?; How much does a 30-day tourist visa cost?; What is the visa fee for Nepal?

ANSWER: Nepal Tourist Visa fees are: 15-day visa: USD 30, 30-day visa: USD 50, 90-day visa: USD 125. Visa extension costs USD 3 per day (minimum 15 days). Multiple re-entry facility costs additional USD 25. Late overstay fine is USD 5 per day. SAARC nationals (except Afghanistan) and children under 10 years get free visa.
