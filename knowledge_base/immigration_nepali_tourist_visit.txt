Nepalese citizens travelling on a Tourist/Visit Visa shall produce the following documents at the departure desk of immigration:
* Valid passport and Visa clarifying the purpose of travel.
* Two-way air-ticket.
* Hotel booking.
* A letter exhibiting details of the sponsor (individual or institution).
* 500 US dollars (cash/dollar or equivalent foreign currency) and authorized stamp/seal of the related bank is required.
* Other documents as appropriate and requested.
If you are traveling to visit your spouse working in Gulf countries, you must produce a recommendation letter issued by the concerned Nepalese Embassy verifying your invitation. Valid Nepali passport and visa are required for the destination country, except where "Visa on Arrival" is available.
