TOPIC: Airport Restrictions
SOURCE: Drone Airport Policies

Access to facilities may be limited in parts, specifically zones within the area of an airport. There are specific areas designated as "no-fly" zones, which include areas around airports.

- Drones are prohibited from flying in and around these restricted zones. This is considered a nationwide and international air traffic hazard.
- No-fly zones in airport facilities extend from a few hundred meters to 5 km in altitude. The values depend upon the particular airport.
- Flying in designated restricted zones (like airports) is illegal and can result in penalties, lawsuits, or imprisonment.
