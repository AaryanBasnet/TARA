Q: Is this rule also applicable when I hike around Kathmandu? Or to Sarangkot? Where all are these rules applicable?
A: The Revised Provision for Trekking in Nepal must be followed only while trekking to areas where TIMS is applicable. It is applicable in all national park areas in the mountains. It is not applicable in Kathmandu Valley outskirts, Pokhara outskirts, and hiking areas in and around major cities.

Q: Do I have to go via a trekking agency? Or is it OK so long as I have a guide?
A: It is mandatory to hire a licensed trekking guide through a government-registered trekking agency.

Q: How can I find that out if the trekking agency is legit?
A: Trekking guide must be hired through government-registered trekking agency. You can verify information on the trekking agency from Department of Tourism and Trekking Agencies Association of Nepal.

Q: What are the consequences if I do not follow this rule?
A: Not following the rule is a punishable offense.
