TOPIC: Immigration Visa On Arrival Type

RELATED QUESTIONS: What are the different kinds of “On Arrival Visa”?; What type of visa is the On Arrival Visa?; Can I get a Business Visa On Arrival?

ANSWER: All 'On Arrival Visas' are Tourist Visas. Tourist Visas are Entry Visas. If you are traveling to Nepal for purposes other than tourism, you will have to change your visa status by contacting the Department of Immigration with the necessary documents.
