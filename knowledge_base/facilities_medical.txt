TOPIC: Facilities Medical

RELATED QUESTIONS: Does the airport have any medical facility?; Is there a doctor or nurse at the airport?; Medical emergency services availability

ANSWER: In the event of a medical emergency, well-trained doctors and nurses are available from the airport medical center run by Kathmandu Medical College (KMC) and HAMS Hospital. They are available round the clock till the operation of the airport and are equipped with emergency treatment centers. Ambulances are provided for severe medical emergencies. This facility is available at both domestic and international terminals.
