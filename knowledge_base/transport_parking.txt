Paid parking facilities are available at the airport. Private cars and taxis can be parked at the Car Park located at the new arrival area of the international terminal. It can also be parked at the Car Park located in front of the operations/airlines building and domestic terminal. Private vehicles coming to pick up guests are given a stay time of the first 5 minutes free, thereafter will be charged as per the rules. However, VIPs and Diplomatic vehicles are advised to park in the parking lot in front of the international terminal.
