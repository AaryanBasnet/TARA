TOPIC: Arrival Lounge Facilities
SOURCE: Arrival Info

Once you step out of the custom area, you are in the arrival lounge, which has the following facilities:

CIP Room (Commercially Important Person Room):
This is available for passengers who wish to rest while someone takes care of their formalities (custom, immigration, and luggage collection).
Cost: Rs 450 per hour per person, including VAT.

Nepal Tourism Board (NTB):
You can acquire information on a host of topics on tourism. You can collect pamphlets and other materials on tourism.

HAN (Hotel Association of Nepal) Counter:
You can book any hotel of your choice from here. Get your hotel voucher and proceed to the assigned hotel.

Pre-paid Taxi:
Once you step out of the arrival gate, the pre-paid taxi service is on your right. You can pay in advance. The rates are made visible as they are exhibited on the board.
