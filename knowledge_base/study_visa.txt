TOPIC: Study Visa
SOURCE: Visa Fee Documents

DESCRIPTION:
- Issued to students pursuing academic / non academic degree and research in Nepal.

ISSUED BY: Department of Immigration

FEE (USD):
- 30 USD/Month for students of students and their dependents (Degree Program).
- 75 USD/Month for students and dependents (Non Degree Program).
- 15 USD/Month for Bhutanese students and their dependents.
- 15 USD/Month for dependents of the students receiving waiver on visa fee as per MoU between Universities.
- 50 USD/Month for foreigners and their dependents for teaching and research purpose.
- 25 USD/Annual for Maldivian and their dependents.
- 15 USD/Annual for Bangladeshi and their dependents.
- Free of Cost for Srilankan and Pakistani Student but 10 USD/Month for their family.
- Additional 20 USD for single re-entry facility.
- Additional 60 USD for multiple re-entry facility.

REQUIRED DOCUMENT:
- Recommendation letter from Ministry of Education
- University / College Admission receipt (original)
- Bank Statement (latest one week) with sufficient funds
- Copies of passport and latest Nepali Visa
- Marriage Certificate and Birth Certificate (for those applying their spouse and children as dependents)
- Progress report Card (for renewal of Student Visa)
- Applicant for the research program should submit an Offer/Appointment letter from Academic Institution of Nepal and General Agreement between two Academic Institutions
- Valid Passport
- Other supporting documents, if required

WHO CAN APPLY & HOW:
- Applicant can apply from the online portal: https://nepaliport.immigration.gov.np/
