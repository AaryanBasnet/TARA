TOPIC: Residential Visa
SOURCE: Visa Fee Documents

DESCRIPTION:
- Issued to the following foreigners and their family intending to spend life in Nepal:
- Persons with an international reputation
- Persons capable of rendering an outstanding contribution to the economic, social and cultural growth of Nepal
- Person who has invested minimum one hundred US dollars in industrial enterprise of Nepal
- Person intending to spend life in Nepal without carrying on any business

ISSUED BY: Department of Immigration

FEE (USD): 1200 USD per year

REQUIRED DOCUMENT:
- For Foreigners who invest minimum of 100 thousands USD:
- Copy of Company registration certificate
- Copy of PAN/VAT certificate
- Copy of the share certificate
- Copy of Industry registration certificate
- Recommendation from Dept. of Industry or Commerce
- Copy of Passport and recent Nepali visa
- Tax clearance certificate for renewal
- Other supporting documents, if required
- For Foreigners who wants to live in Nepal without doing any business:
- Bank statement of USD 20,000
- Certificate having regular income of at least USD 20,000 per year (verified/attested by the concerned embassy)
- Character Certificate
- No Objection letter from concerned country of Embassy
- Health certificate issued by TU Teaching Hospital
- The evidence of completing 60 years or proof of retirement
- Copy of valid passport and recent Nepali visa
- Expenditure statement of USD 20,000 for renewal
- Valid Passport, if required
- Other supporting documents, if required

WHO CAN APPLY & HOW:
- Applicant can apply from the online portal: https://nepaliport.immigration.gov.np/
