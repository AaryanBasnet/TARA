Day 8:  Trek down to Birethanti and drive to Pokhara
Spend a free day in Pokhara. 
You have options to go for some additional Adventure activities
because Pokhara City is the Adventure Tourism Hub of Nepal. Some of
the interesting activities are as follows. 1. Charter Helicopter flight to
Mt. Annapurna Base Camp or Mardi Himal Base Camp. 2. Ultra-light
flight. 3. Paragliding. 4.Zip Line. 5.Bungee Jump   
Day 9:  In Pokhara  
Day 10:  Fly to Kathmandu 
After breakfast, transfers to Pokhara Domestic Airport and take a
scenic 25 minutes flight back to Kathmandu. 
Upon arrival in Kathmandu transfer to hotel for Check-In. Walk around
beautiful Thamel Bazaar.
