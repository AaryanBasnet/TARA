TOPIC: Customs Gold Jewelry

RELATED QUESTIONS: Can I allowed to bring gold jewelry items?; Duty-free limit for gold jewelry for male and female passengers; Maximum allowed weight for gold jewelry with customs duty

ANSWER: Normally, it is allowed to bring 50 grams of gold jewelry for female and 25 grams for male without paying customs duty. On top of this, you are allowed to bring up to 100 grams with customs duty and another 100 grams with extra 3% charges on the applicable customs duty. Gold jewelry more than 225 grams for male and 250 grams for female are not allowed in any case and will be seized. *Note: Beruwa rings, Bangles, chain like jewelry made from 24 carat gold (free from alloy) which looks like ornaments will not be considered as ornaments.* For more information, visit https://customs.gov.np/
