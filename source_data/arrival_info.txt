Topic: Arrival Hall Facilities
The arrival hall has the following facilities:
- Himalaya Money Exchange
- Yeti Money Exchange
It is advisable you change your money here. Remember to obtain your receipt of the amount you have changed.

Topic: Customs at Arrival

There are two channels for customs clearance: the Green Channel and the Red Channel.

Green Channel:
Passengers not having any dutiable items can proceed to the Green Channel.
Passengers having the following items (within the specified limits) may use the Green Channel:
- Gold ornaments not more than 50 gms
- Silver ornaments not more than 500 gms
- Used linen
- One camera
- One pair of binoculars
- One movie/video camera
- One radio
- One tape recorder
- 15 tape cassettes
- 10 disc recorders
- 15 rolls of photo films
- 10 rolls of movie camera films
- One tricycle
- One perambulator
- One wristwatch
- Necessary medicines
- Ordinary packed food worth not more than NRs 10,000
- Fresh fruit worth not more than NRs 1,000

Red Channel:
If a passenger is in doubt about dutiable or non-dutiable items, he/she can proceed to the Red Channel.

Currency Declaration:
Passengers should declare foreign currency if they have more than US$ 5,000 or its equivalent in any convertible currency.

Rules for Electronic Equipment:
All-electric equipment, TV, Video Camera, Laptop, etc. should be either duty paid or endorsed on your passport so that it can be carried back to your country after completing your stay.

Topic: Immigration Counter

Visas are required for all foreigners wishing to enter Nepal, except for Indian nationalities, who need to show their identity at the counter to identify themselves.

There is a special counter for VIPs/Diplomats.

It is advisable that you fill up the disembarkation card in the aircraft itself before disembarking to avoid wasting time filling it at the arrival counter.

TIA Immigration can grant you a VISA for 90 days. For further details, you can contact the Immigration Office at Dillibazar, Kalikasthan in Kathmandu.

After visa formalities are completed, you have to go downstairs to collect your luggage at the custom area.

You can make a local call from the telephone booth behind the Immigration counter before descending the steps into the Custom area.

You need to get your hand baggage examined through the X-ray machine before your exit to the arrival lounge.

Free baggage trolleys are available.

Topic: Arrival Lounge Facilities

Once you step out of the custom area, you are in the arrival lounge, which has the following facilities:

CIP Room (Commercially Important Person Room):
This is available for passengers who wish to rest while someone takes care of their formalities (custom, immigration, and luggage collection).
Cost: Rs 450 per hour per person, including VAT.

Nepal Tourism Board (NTB):
You can acquire information on a host of topics on tourism. You can collect pamphlets and other materials on tourism.

HAN (Hotel Association of Nepal) Counter:
You can book any hotel of your choice from here. Get your hotel voucher and proceed to the assigned hotel.

Pre-paid Taxi:
Once you step out of the arrival gate, the pre-paid taxi service is on your left. You can pay in advance. The rates are made visible as they are exhibited on the board.
