Topic: Free Wifi
Free wi-fi service is available at International Terminal Building from departure area to arrival. Passenger can enjoy the facility of free wi-fi service. They can easily connect to wifi-(Free_CAAN_Worldlink_Wifi) on their device and enjoy the internet service. Beside this, there is another free wifi service(TIA-Wifi-Departure) available after passing through immigration area of departure.

---
Topic: CIP Lounge
Available by reservation for business travelers. This is available for Commercially important person who on arrival may wish to rest while someone takes care of their formalities. You can available this room after paying specified amount per hour, per person including VAT.

---
Topic: Trolley Services
If you are departing from International or Domestic Terminal, you will find plenty of trolleys at the upper and lower curbside areas outside the terminals. If you are arriving, you will find trolleys at the baggage claim area. Use of trolley is free of charge.

---
Topic: Baby Feeding Room
This facility is available at departure sterile hall to provide comfortable services for breast feeding persons.

---
Topic: Baggage Wrapping Services
Baggage wrapping service is available at departure hall. It provides facilities to passenger for wrapping their luggage. This Baggage Wrapping service is situated in front of check in counter 'A'.

---
Topic: Banking Facilities
You will find a host of international and local banks offering ATMs for cash withdrawals, transfers and deposits, as well as some branches which provide full transactional support for all your banking needs. With Specialist currency exchange provides, you are never far from this essential service at Tribhuvan International Airport. Several local banks also offer foreign exchange service at reasonable rates. 

---
Topic: Restaurants and Cafeteria
Executive Lounge is situated at departure hall of international terminal building, first floor after passing through immigration. Radisson Hotel is located at first floor of departure hall of international terminal building.Beside these, there are various tea/coffee shops. Horizon lounge run by Soaltee Hotel Group is also available here.

---
Topic: Parking
In front of operation/airline building, 2206.17 square meter area is allocated for parking. In this area, parking facility available for two wheeler vehicle at a time is 350 Numbers and parking facility available for four wheeler vehicles at a time is 100 Numbers. In domestic, 5142.75 square meter area is allocated for parking. Beside these, there are also free parking area and other open spaces for prepaid taxi and other vehicles

---
Topic: Medical Services
Medical service provides diagnosis, first-aid and ambulance services (admitting and transferring patients) in the terminal and prepares all kinds of first-aid medicine and common pharmaceuticals. If you feel unwell or require medical assistance while in Airport, you may go to the KMC (Kathmandu Medical College) or HAMS, where the service is available at first floor of departure hall.

---
Topic: Police Help Desk
This desk is situated outside International Terminal Building which provides information about various queries like check in terminals, wheel chair facility, flight schedule etc.

---
Topic: Pre-paid Taxi Service
Once you step out of the arrival gate the pre-paid taxi service is on your right if you wish to use this facility you can do so by paying in advance. The rates are made visible as they are exhibited on the board for your security.

---
Topic: Foreign Exchange
There is one bank counter at the departure lounge and two money exchange counters at the arrival lounge. All transactions are done on the basis of the declared amount by the passenger and the currencies permitted by the government based on the day's foreign exchange rate. 

---
Topic: Information Desk
Information desk provide information about flight related queries, check-in counters, terminals, information about other services required for passenger etc. Passenger can find this service at departure hall near check in counters. Help desk location canopy area, before entering into the terminal.

---
Topic: Baggage Help Desk
Passenger can find information regarding their baggage at baggage help desk, which is located at arrival hall (baggage area). Passenger can report their problem if they can't find their luggage at estimated time.

---
Topic: Charging Hub
Charging hubs are available at various places of departure and arrival lounge in order to give easy access to passengers

---
Topic: Complaint Box
Complaint boxes are available at various places of departure area where passengers can drop their complaint about services available in airport.

---
Topic: Tea & Confectionery Shop
Tea and Confectionery shop are available at various places of departure area to serve high quality products to international travelers.

---
Topic: Food Court & Lounge
Food Court & Lounge, which is conveniently located near the International terminal departure gate, just past the VIP entrance. Thoughtfully designed with the Boutique-inspired theme to ensure comfort and ease, it offers a wide range of services including a relaxing lounge area, secure luggage storage & a multi-cuisine dining experience in a calm and welcoming environment.
Guests can enjoy a variety of freshly prepared dishes, light snacks, and beverages featuring both authentic Thakali, Nepali, and continental cuisine.
