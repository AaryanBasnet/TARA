Topic: Before Touch Down Charges
TIA is willingly emerging as a major commercial entity while keeping itself to the role of infrastructure provider for airlines and other clients.

Following are the list of charges category for both domestic and international flight operator:
- Landing Charges for Int. Flight
- Parking Charges for Int. Flight
- Navigation Charges for Int. Flight
- Landing Charges for Dom. Flight
- Parking Charges for Dom. Flight
- Security Service Charge
- Charter Charge
- Other Charges

---
Topic: Landing Charges - Domestic
When an aircraft is landed in this airport the operator should pay the landing charge according to the following table.

- Upto 10,000 Kgs: Rs. 55.00 (Per 1000 Kgs)
- 10,001 Kgs - 25,000 Kgs: Rs. 550.00 (First 10,000 Kgs) + Rs. 110.00 (After 10,000 Per 1000)

---
Topic: Landing Charges - International
When an aircraft is landed in this airport the operator should pay the landing charge according to the following table.

- Upto 10,000 Kgs: US$ 1.25 (Per 1000 Kgs)
- 10,001 Kgs - 25,000 Kgs: US$ 12.25 (First 10,000 Kgs) + US$ 2.50 (After 10,000 Per 1000)
- 25,001 Kgs - 50,000 Kgs: US$ 49.00 (First 25,000 Kgs) + US$ 3.75 (After 25,000 Per 1000)
- 50,001 Kgs - 75,000 Kgs: US$ 140.50 (First 50,000 Kgs) + US$ 6.00 (After 50,000 Per 1000)
- 75,001 Kgs - 1,00,000 Kgs: US$ 293.25 (First 75,000 Kgs) + US$ 7.35 (After 75,000 Per 1000)
- Above 1,00,000 Kgs: US$ 475.00 (First 1,00,000 Kgs) + US$ 8.55 (After 1,00,000 Per 1000)

Topic: Parking Charges - Domestic
When an aircraft is parked no Parking Charges shall be levied for the first 3 hrs. Provided that the period of parking exceeds 3 hrs, then 6 each hrs will make one parking unit.

- Upto 40,000 Kgs: Rs. 37.50 (Per 1000 Kgs)
- 40,001 Kgs - 1,00,000 Kgs: Rs. 1500.00 (First 40,000 Kgs) + Rs. 54.00 (After 40,000 Per 1000)
- Above 1,00,000 Kgs: Rs. 4740.00 (First 1,00,000 Kgs) + Rs. 75.00 (After 1,00,000 Per 1000)
