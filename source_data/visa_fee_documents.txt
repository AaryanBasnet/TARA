Topic: Diplomatic Visa
DESCRIPTION:
- Issued to a person holding a diplomatic passport issued from any country and recommended from the concerned diplomatic mission for the diplomatic visa.
- Issued to a person considered eligible to grant diplomatic privileges by Government of Nepal (GoN) and the family members of such person holding the diplomatic passport.

ISSUED BY:
- Department of Consular Services
- 30-Days entry visa is issued by Embassies of Nepal or by Immigration offices at entry points.

FEE (USD): Free of Cost

REQUIRED DOCUMENT:
- Valid Diplomatic Passport
- Diplomatic Mission Note and Recommendation
- Other supporting documents, if required

WHO CAN APPLY & HOW:
- Diplomats and their dependents with diplomatic passports can apply to the Department of Consular Services via: https://nepalconsular.gov.np/diplomatic/public/
- Diplomats and their dependents with diplomatic passports can apply for 30 days entry visa from the online portal: https://nepaliport.immigration.gov.np/

Topic: Tourist Visa
DESCRIPTION:
- Issued to foreigners travelling to Nepal as an entry visa (On-Arrival Visa) at the port of entry.
- Issued as an entry visa by Diplomatic Missions abroad.
- Tourist visa is extended not exceeding total 150 days of stay in a single visa year (Jan-Dec).

ISSUED BY:
- 15/30/90-Days entry visa is issued by Immigration Offices.
- Tourist Visa extension is done for minimum 15 days from Immigration Offices and Department of Immigration.
- 15/30/90-Days entry visa is issued by Diplomatic Missions.

FEE (USD):
- Entry Visa 15 Days: USD 30
- Entry Visa 30 Days: USD 50
- Entry Visa 90 Days: USD 125
- Visa Extension: 3 USD per Day (minimum 15 days)
- Late Fine: 5 USD per day as late fine in the case of delay
- Multiple re-entry facility: Additional 25 USD

REQUIRED DOCUMENT:
- Valid Ordinary Passport
- Previous Visa, if any
- Other supporting documents, if required

WHO CAN APPLY & HOW:
- Foreigners can apply from the online portal: https://nepaliport.immigration.gov.np/

Topic: Non-Tourist Visa - Working Visa
DESCRIPTION:
- Issued to foreign nationals only upon the recommendation from line agency (concerned Ministry of GoN), Labour Permit from the Department of Labour and Work Agreement from the Ministry of Home Affairs.

ISSUED BY: Department of Immigration

FEE (USD):
- 30 USD/Month: Foreigners working in offices or project directly affiliated with GoN and their dependents.
- 50 USD/Month: Foreigners and their dependants working in organizations having contract between donor country and the Nepal Government and funded by the donor country.
- 75 USD/Month: Foreigners and their dependents working in any organizations.
- Additional 20 USD for single re-entry facility.
- Additional 60 USD for multiple re-entry facility.

REQUIRED DOCUMENT:
- Recommendation letter
- Work / Labor Permit
- Work Agreement
- Appointment/ Contract letter
- Tax Clearance Certificate of the hiring organization
- Tax Clearance certificate of an applicant (for renewal)
- General Agreement with Social Welfare Council (NGOs/INGOs)
- Valid Passport
- Other supporting documents, if required

WHO CAN APPLY & HOW:
- Foreigners can apply from the online portal: https://nepaliport.immigration.gov.np/

Topic: Non-Tourist Visa - Relation Visa
DESCRIPTION:
- Issued to foreigners having direct relation with Nepalese.
- Issued to relatives of foreigners having marital relation with Nepali citizen.

ISSUED BY: Department of Immigration

FEE (USD):
- 10 USD per month for foreigners having direct relation with Nepalese.
- 25 USD per month for relatives of foreigners having marital relation with Nepali citizen.
- Additional 20 USD for single re-entry facility.
- Additional 60 USD for multiple re-entry facility.

REQUIRED DOCUMENT:
- Valid Passport
- Relation Certificate issued by local bodies establishing relationship with Nepali kin (related to applicant by blood) along with supporting documents to verify their relationship.
- Citizenship Certificate of kin
- Relation Verifying other documents such as birth certificate
- Physical presence of the kin for the verification formalities

WHO CAN APPLY & HOW:
- Applicant can apply from the online portal: https://nepaliport.immigration.gov.np/

Topic: Non-Tourist Visa - Marriage Visa
DESCRIPTION:
- Issued to foreigners married to Nepali citizen.

ISSUED BY: Department of Immigration

FEE (USD):
- 15 USD per month
- No Visa fee till marriage relation for foreigners who took visa free of cost according to Foreign Relation Regulation, 2032.
- Additional 20 USD for single re-entry facility.
- Additional 60 USD for multiple re-entry facility.

REQUIRED DOCUMENT:
- Valid Passport
- Marriage Certificate issued by District Administration Office or District Court. If marriage registered abroad; Marriage Registration Certificate and verification letter from concerned embassy.
- Citizenship Certificate of Nepali spouse
- Physical presence of Nepali spouse
- Marriage registration certificate from concerned local level (in case of court marriage, marriage done from diplomatic missions from/in Nepal and marriage done in abroad between nepali and foreign citizen).
- Marriage certificate from local level

WHO CAN APPLY & HOW:
- Applicant can apply from the online portal: https://nepaliport.immigration.gov.np/

Topic: Study Visa
DESCRIPTION:
- Issued to students pursuing academic / non academic degree and research in Nepal.

ISSUED BY: Department of Immigration

FEE (USD):
- 30 USD/Month for students of students and their dependents (Degree Program).
- 75 USD/Month for students and dependents (Non Degree Program).
- 15 USD/Month for Bhutanese students and their dependents.
- 15 USD/Month for dependents of the students receiving waiver on visa fee as per MoU between Universities.
- 50 USD/Month for foreigners and their dependents for teaching and research purpose.
- 25 USD/Annual for Maldivian and their dependents.
- 15 USD/Annual for Bangladeshi and their dependents.
- Free of Cost for Srilankan and Pakistani Student but 10 USD/Month for their family.
- Additional 20 USD for single re-entry facility.
- Additional 60 USD for multiple re-entry facility.

REQUIRED DOCUMENT:
- Recommendation letter from Ministry of Education
- University / College Admission receipt (original)
- Bank Statement (latest one week) with sufficient funds
- Copies of passport and latest Nepali Visa
- Marriage Certificate and Birth Certificate (for those applying their spouse and children as dependents)
- Progress report Card (for renewal of Student Visa)
- Applicant for the research program should submit an Offer/Appointment letter from Academic Institution of Nepal and General Agreement between two Academic Institutions
- Valid Passport
- Other supporting documents, if required

WHO CAN APPLY & HOW:
- Applicant can apply from the online portal: https://nepaliport.immigration.gov.np/

Topic: Residential Visa
DESCRIPTION:
- Issued to the following foreigners and their family intending to spend life in Nepal:
- Persons with an international reputation
- Persons capable of rendering an outstanding contribution to the economic, social and cultural growth of Nepal
- Person who has invested minimum one hundred US dollars in industrial enterprise of Nepal
- Person intending to spend life in Nepal without carrying on any business

ISSUED BY: Department of Immigration

FEE (USD): 1200 USD per year

REQUIRED DOCUMENT:
- For Foreigners who invest minimum of 100 thousands USD:
 - Copy of Company registration certificate
 - Copy of PAN/VAT certificate
 - Copy of the share certificate
 - Copy of Industry registration certificate
 - Recommendation from Dept. of Industry or Commerce
 - Copy of Passport and recent Nepali visa
 - Tax clearance certificate for renewal
 - Other supporting documents, if required
- For Foreigners who wants to live in Nepal without doing any business:
 - Bank statement of USD 20,000
 - Certificate having regular income of at least USD 20,000 per year (verified/attested by the concerned embassy)
 - Character Certificate
 - No Objection letter from concerned country of Embassy
 - Health certificate issued by TU Teaching Hospital
 - The evidence of completing 60 years or proof of retirement
 - Copy of valid passport and recent Nepali visa
 - Expenditure statement of USD 20,000 for renewal
 - Valid Passport, if required
 - Other supporting documents, if required

WHO CAN APPLY & HOW:
- Applicant can apply from the online portal: https://nepaliport.immigration.gov.np/

Topic: Gratis Visa (Visa for Free)
DESCRIPTION:
- Gratis Visa (Visa for Free) is issued free of cost for following categories of Visa applicants:
- Children below 10 years except US citizens
- Up to 30 days for SAARC Citizen (except Afghanistan) visiting Nepal for the first time in a given visa year. Afghan citizens are eligible for Gratis Visa on Arrival only upon the recommendation of Department of Immigration.
- Non Residential Nepalese (NRN) card holder
- Chinese Nationals are eligible for 150 days tourist Gratis Visa in a given visa year
