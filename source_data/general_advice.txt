---
Topic: When to Arrive at Airport
We recommend passengers arrive at the airport 3 hours before their international flight. The check-in commencement time is usually 3 hours prior to departure, and the ticket checking counters close at least one hour prior to departure.
---
Topic: How early should I get to the airport
For international flights, it is best to arrive at the airport 3 hours before your flight's scheduled departure time. This gives you time for check-in, immigration, and security.
---
Topic: General Travel Preparation
Before you leave for the airport, please double-check that you have all your necessary travel documents. This includes your passport, visa (if required), and flight ticket or boarding pass.
---
Topic: Check Your Documents
Ensure your passport is valid for at least six months beyond your travel date, as this is a requirement for many countries. Also, confirm all visa requirements for your destination and any transit countries.
---
Topic: Baggage Allowance
Baggage allowances vary greatly between airlines. To avoid extra fees, please check your baggage weight and size limits with your airline directly before arriving at the airport.
---
Topic: Packing Valuables
For your safety, it is highly recommended that you pack all valuable items in your hand baggage (carry-on), not in your checked luggage. This includes items like laptops, cameras, jewelry, important documents, and any required medications.
---
Topic: Label Your Luggage
Make sure all your bags, including both checked and carry-on luggage, are clearly labeled with your name and contact information (like a phone number or email address).
---
Topic: General Security Advice
For your safety and the safety of others, never leave your baggage unattended at the airport. Do not accept any items from unknown persons.
---
Topic: Suspicious Activity
If you notice any suspicious activity or unattended baggage, please report it immediately to airport security personnel or the nearest help desk.
---
Topic: At the Check-in Counter
Have your passport and ticket ready when you approach the check-in counter. Be aware of the baggage allowance rules for your specific airline.
---
Topic: Flight Information Screens (FIDS)
After checking in, always check the Flight Information Display Screens (FIDS) located throughout the terminal. These screens have the most up-to-date information on your flight's status, boarding time, and gate number.
---
Topic: Boarding Time
Please proceed to your assigned boarding gate well in advance of your flight's departure. Airlines may close the boarding gate 20-30 minutes before the flight is scheduled to leave.
---
Topic: Health and Wellness
Air travel can be tiring. Remember to stay hydrated by drinking water and move or stretch your legs when possible during your flight.
---
Topic: Arriving at TIA
When you arrive in Kathmandu, please follow the signs for Immigration. Have your passport and any required visa or arrival card ready. After immigration, you can proceed to the baggage claim area.
---
Topic: Customs Declaration
After collecting your luggage, you will pass through customs. Be sure to declare any items as required by Nepal's customs regulations. If you are unsure, please proceed to the 'Red Channel' to speak with a customs officer.
---
Topic: Ground Transportation
For your safety, it is recommended to use the official "Pre-paid Taxi" service or other registered transport services when leaving the airport.
---
Topic: What to do if your baggage is lost
If your baggage is lost or damaged, please contact your airline's baggage service counter immediately, *before* you leave the baggage claim area.
---
Topic: What to do if you lose an item
If you lose a personal item within the airport terminal, please contact the TIA "Lost & Found" unit as soon as possible.
